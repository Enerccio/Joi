# Profiles

A profile is a place she is in: home, work. Each profile has its own **body** and its own state of that body's **parts and
outfit**. You assign each of your nodes to one profile, so the home PC and the phone can show her in a dressing gown while the
work laptop shows her in a suit.

Without profiles she has one body state and every node shows it. That is the **default profile**; it is the state her bodies
keep. You do not need profiles unless you want different looks in different places.

## Making them

Open her dialog and go to the **Profiles** tab.

- **Add profile** asks for a name. A new profile starts as the default one is: the same body, the same parts and outfit.
- The pencil renames a profile (the default one too); the bin removes it. A removed profile's nodes are in the default one again.
- **Nodes** lists every node that has connected to her, by the name it gave, with a mark while it is connected. Choose its profile in
  the list. A node that is in none is in the default profile. The bin forgets a node that is away.

## Changing one

In the **Bodies** tab a **Profile** choice appears once she has more than one. What you do there is done in that profile: the
body she is in (the arrow), the body dialog's parts and outfits. The other profiles do not follow.

A node shows what its own profile has. When you change a profile, only its nodes read her body again. A part or an outfit
you switch on a node changes the profile of that node.

## What she knows

She is one person with one conversation, and she knows where she is:

- Her prompt says which profile she is in (when there is more than one) and shows how she looks in it.
- When a message comes from a node in another profile than her last turn was in, she is told: "You were in *Home* until now;
  you are in *Work* now, and here you look as follows." She can react, or say nothing.
- A change in a profile she is not in is not told as it happens: she finds out when she moves there.
- What she does herself (her functions for her parts, outfits and bodies) changes the profile of the node she is talking
  through.
- Something that happens while you are away, like a task of hers that ended, is told on the node you used last, and she is in the
  profile of that node. If no node is connected it waits.
